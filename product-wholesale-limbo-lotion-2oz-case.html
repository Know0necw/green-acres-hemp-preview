Our story

How Limbo Lotion started

We got our start because of a friend of ours who was in Vietnam and came home with Agent Orange. The only thing, over the years, that he had found to help was a cream the doctors prescribed — he could only use it twice a day, it had a shelf life of one month, it only stopped the itching and never healed the problem, and it cost him $500 a month. My wife Lisa and I have been experimenting with hemp and hemp oils over a period of time and came up with a fragrance-free, all-natural lotion infused with hemp oil. That’s when Limbo Lotion was born.
