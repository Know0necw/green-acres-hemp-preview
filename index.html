A powerful plant

Outlawed in the 1930s. Grown here since 2014.

Industrial hemp was mandatory for Midwest farmers during the war — rope, sails, clothing. The Declaration of Independence was written on hemp paper, as was the first Bible. Hemp is a cousin of marijuana but contains little or no THC. We grow licensed industrial hemp at 0.3% THC or lower.

Legalized again in 2014, hemp is used for houses, biodiesel, clothing, medicines, beauty, and even car interiors. We are helping in the fight to make this a healthier, greener place to live — for us, for you, and for the generations after.
