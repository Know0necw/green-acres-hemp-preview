How Limbo Lotion started

Our story — Vietnam, Agent Orange, and a $500 cream

We got our start because of a friend of ours who was in Vietnam and came home with Agent Orange. The only thing, over the years, that he had found to help was a cream the doctors prescribed — he could only use it twice a day, it had a shelf life of one month, it only stopped the itching and never healed the problem, and it cost him $500 a month.

My wife Lisa and I have been experimenting with hemp and hemp oils over a period of time and came up with a fragrance-free, all-natural lotion infused with hemp oil. That's when Limbo Lotion was born.

Green Acres Hemp Farm is a commercially licensed industrial hemp farm in beautiful Alamosa, Colorado. We grow, make, and sell all of our products from hemp and the hemp leaf. Everything we grow and use in our products is organic. Our oils test at 0.3% THC. We have sent people down for DOT drug testing — they ingested twice the recommended dose daily for six weeks before the test. We have never had a positive. Nobody has ever flunked a drug test ingesting our products since we opened in 2014.

We are A-rated with the Better Business Bureau. We now sell infused CBD in organic MCT oil in capsules and tincture, alongside olive-oil formulas, Limbo Lotion, lip balm, pet products, and hemp sandals. Thank you for all the support — have a great day from Green Acres Hemp Farm.
